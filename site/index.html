From a pile of sources
to a living scenario.

01

Ground the question

Bring the reports, articles, and notes that matter. Flock keeps claims linked to their source.

02

Map the relationships

Your host’s active model extracts people, groups, events, and connections into a graph you can inspect and question.

03

Scenario Lab: compare possibilities

Shape a synthetic community, then compare a baseline with focused alternatives across repeated, isolated runs.

04

Evidence Audit: trace claims to sources

Trace factual claims to short source excerpts. Review what is observed, inferred, or assumed—and where sources disagree.

Built around your host session

No Flock key.
Your host, your data.

Flock does not collect model credentials. Sign in or configure the AI host you choose; that host’s provider settings, data policies, plan, and limits apply. Flock’s SQLite graph and simulation records stay in your selected workspace, without Zep or a Flock-hosted data service.
